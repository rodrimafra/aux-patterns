# Wayfinders

> Contextual cues and suggestions that reveal what the agent could do next, inside the flow of work.

## Overview

Blank-canvas agents fail because capability is invisible. Wayfinders surface timely, situation-aware suggestions, not a manual, but signposts placed exactly where the user already is.

## Why it works

Discovery in context costs nothing; discovery in documentation costs a trip nobody takes. Suggestion chips double as capability disclosure.

## When to use it

- Conversational interfaces with broad, invisible capability
- First-run and empty states
- Moments after a completed task, to chain the next one

## When to avoid it

- Interruptive suggestions during focused work, wayfinding must never become advertising

## Examples in the wild

- ChatGPT's suggested prompts on new chats
- Notion AI's contextual command menu
- Gmail's contextual nudges (follow up, reply suggestions)

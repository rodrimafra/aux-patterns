# Scenario templates & recipes

> Pre-built, editable starting workflows that encode what the agent does well.

## Overview

A template is a capability claim you can run. Recipes package proven multi-step workflows (inputs, steps, expected outputs) so users start from working examples instead of blank prompts.

## Why it works

Templates transfer expertise from the few who explore to the many who adopt. They also constrain early use to paths the agent handles reliably.

## When to use it

- Broad agents whose best uses are non-obvious
- Team settings where workflows should be shared
- Reducing variance in output quality

## When to avoid it

- When templates ossify into the only ways users ever engage

## Examples in the wild

- Zapier's AI workflow templates
- The GPT Store as a recipe marketplace
- Notion template gallery applied to AI workflows

# Sentiment-aware response styles

> The agent reads frustration and adapts: shorter, humbler, faster to escalate.

## Overview

Nothing enrages like chirpy obliviousness after a third failure. This pattern tunes tone and behaviour to the user's state (dropping the flourishes, acknowledging the miss, offering the exit) without pretending to feelings it doesn't have.

## Why it works

De-escalation preserves the relationship through the failure. The behavioural changes matter more than the tone: frustrated users get brevity, options, and escalation, not sympathy theatre.

## When to use it

- Repeated failures within one session
- Customer-facing agents in emotional domains
- Moments before rage-quit, detectable in interaction signals

## When to avoid it

- Simulated emotional intimacy, adaptation must stay honest

## Examples in the wild

- De-escalation modes in support bots
- Terser retry behaviour after repeated corrections
- Fast-tracked human handoff on detected frustration

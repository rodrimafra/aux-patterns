# Streaming results visualizations

> Output renders progressively as it is produced, making pace and direction visible.

## Overview

Streaming is more than perceived speed. Watching output form lets users judge direction early (and interrupt early) turning generation from a black box into an observable process.

## Why it works

Early visibility enables early correction, and progressive rendering keeps engagement through waits that a blank screen would lose.

## When to use it

- Generative text, code, and imagery
- Long-form outputs users may want to redirect
- Paired with steerable interruption

## When to avoid it

- Outputs only meaningful when complete (final totals, verdicts)

## Examples in the wild

- Token streaming across chat products
- Midjourney's progressive image resolve
- Live-updating result tables in research agents

# Fleet health dashboard

> Organisation-level monitoring of all deployed agents: activity, performance, cost, anomalies.

## Overview

Ten agents are a team; a thousand are infrastructure. The fleet dashboard gives operators the aggregate view (throughput, error rates, spend, drift) with drill-down from anomaly to individual session.

## Why it works

Organisations delegate at scale only when someone can answer 'are the agents okay?' at a glance. Observability is the price of fleet autonomy.

## When to use it

- Enterprise deployments beyond a handful of agents
- Cost governance of model usage
- Detecting degradation and drift early

## When to avoid it

- Solo products, where a usage page covers it

## Examples in the wild

- LLM observability platforms (traces, evals, cost)
- Copilot usage analytics for organisations
- SRE dashboards as the design ancestor

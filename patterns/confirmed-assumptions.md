# Confirmed assumptions

> The agent states the assumptions it is proceeding on, giving users a chance to correct them without blocking.

## Overview

Silent assumptions are landmines; blocking questions are speed bumps. The middle path: declare assumptions inline ('Assuming Q3 means calendar Q3') and proceed, unless the user objects.

## Why it works

Work continues at full speed while every assumption remains visible and correctable. Errors surface at the moment of divergence instead of at delivery.

## When to use it

- Ambiguity with one strongly probable reading
- Time-sensitive tasks where blocking is costly
- Long tasks where assumptions compound

## When to avoid it

- High-stakes forks where a wrong assumption is expensive (ask instead

## Examples in the wild

- Stated defaults in booking flows ('economy, 1 adult) change?')
- Agents noting interpretation choices in their working output
- Auto-detected settings with visible override

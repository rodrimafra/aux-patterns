# Workflow & policy template library

> A curated library of approved agent workflows and policies, reusable across the organisation.

## Overview

Every team reinventing agent governance produces inconsistency at best. The library packages vetted workflows (with their permissions, gates, and escalation rules baked in) as the paved road teams actually want to take.

## Why it works

Templates make the compliant path the easy path. Governance embedded in reusable artifacts scales; governance in documents does not.

## When to use it

- Multi-team organisations standardising agent practice
- Compliance requirements needing consistent enforcement
- Accelerating new-team adoption safely

## When to avoid it

- A library without ownership rots into a liability

## Examples in the wild

- Terraform modules as governance-as-code prior art
- Shared workflow templates in automation platforms
- Enterprise prompt and policy libraries

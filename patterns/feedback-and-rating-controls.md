# Feedback & rating controls [Improve this pattern]

> Lightweight in-flow mechanisms for users to grade agent output, feeding visible improvement.

## Overview

Thumbs, stars, accept/reject, the signal matters less than the loop. This pattern captures judgement at the moment of use and, critically, shows users that their feedback changed something.

## Why it works

Feedback that visibly improves behaviour converts users from consumers into trainers, deepening investment. Silent feedback boxes teach people to stop bothering.

## When to use it

- Any generative output a user can judge instantly
- Systems that personalise per user or per team
- Early-stage products hunting failure modes

## When to avoid it

- High-friction surveys where a one-tap signal would do

## Examples in the wild

- ChatGPT response ratings
- Copilot's accept/dismiss as implicit signal
- Midjourney's image ranking feeding model preferences

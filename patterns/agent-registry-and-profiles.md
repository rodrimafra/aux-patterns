# Agent registry & profiles

> A browsable directory of available agents: capabilities, permissions, track record, ownership.

## Overview

As agents multiply, 'which one do I use?' becomes a real question. The registry answers it with structured profiles, what each agent does, what it may access, how it has performed, who maintains it.

## Why it works

Discoverability plus accountability. A registry turns an opaque agent population into a staffed team with named responsibilities.

## When to use it

- Organisations running many specialised agents
- Marketplaces of third-party agents
- Governance regimes requiring ownership records

## When to avoid it

- A registry nobody curates becomes a graveyard that misleads

## Examples in the wild

- The GPT Store's capability profiles
- Slack app directory with scopes and publishers
- Internal agent catalogues in enterprise platforms

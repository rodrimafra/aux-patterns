# Explanation on demand

> Any output can be interrogated: 'why this?' produces the reasoning, sources, and trade-offs behind it.

## Overview

Explanations shouldn't be forced on every result, and shouldn't be unavailable when wanted. On-demand explanation keeps the default clean while guaranteeing that the reasoning behind any output is one gesture away.

## Why it works

Optional depth serves both the trusting and the sceptical without taxing either. The affordance's mere presence builds confidence, most users never tap it, but everyone knows they could.

## When to use it

- Recommendations and rankings
- Surprising or counterintuitive outputs
- Expert users auditing agent judgement

## When to avoid it

- Burying legally required disclosures behind an optional tap

## Examples in the wild

- 'Because you watched…' rationales in recommenders
- Reason codes in credit decisions
- Ask-to-explain follow-ups in chat assistants

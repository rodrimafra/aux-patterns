# Apology & remedy bundle

> After a real failure: acknowledgment, plain explanation, concrete fix, and what will prevent recurrence, as one unit.

## Overview

An apology without a remedy is PR; a fix without acknowledgment is gaslighting. The bundle delivers all four parts together, sized to the harm, a slipped deadline gets a sentence; corrupted work gets the full treatment.

## Why it works

Service-recovery research is unambiguous: failures handled this way can leave more trust than no failure at all. Users don't expect perfection; they expect ownership.

## When to use it

- Failures with real user cost
- Errors the user will discover anyway, get there first
- Recurring failures, where the prevention clause is the point

## When to avoid it

- Reflexive apologising for trivia, which cheapens the currency

## Examples in the wild

- Status-page postmortems: impact, cause, prevention
- Proactive service recovery (refund before the complaint)
- Agents reporting their own failure with remediation options

# Progressive disclosure modes [Review this pattern]

> Advanced autonomy and capability revealed gradually, matched to the user's demonstrated skill and trust.

## Overview

The full power of an agent on day one is noise. This pattern sequences capability: simple assistance first, deeper autonomy unlocked as usage signals readiness, mirroring how responsibility is granted to a new colleague.

## Why it works

Cognitive load stays manageable and trust grows on evidence. Users who never need the advanced tier never pay its complexity tax.

## When to use it

- Feature-rich agents serving mixed skill levels
- Autonomy that is dangerous in untrained hands
- Long-lived products where relationships deepen

## When to avoid it

- Expert tools where gating frustrates users who arrive skilled

## Examples in the wild

- Copilot features rolling out behind usage milestones
- Advanced settings hidden behind progressive menus
- Gradual autonomy unlocks in finance apps

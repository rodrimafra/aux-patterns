Editorial Geometry is a typesetter's algorithm for stills. It does not depict a workplace, a tool, or a body. It composes a poster the way a cover is composed: a field, a counterweight, a force, a single accent, and a lot of unused paper. The craft target is Laws of UX adjacency: one idea, readable at a glance beside a title. Someone who knows the category question may feel it in how masses relate. Everyone else should still meet a calm, inevitable arrangement. This is the product of deep computational expertise applied as restraint, not as spectacle.

The alphabet is one across the library. Primitives are few: circle, capsule, rounded rectangle, rectangle, triangle, thin rule, quarter-arc. Coordinates and sizes snap to an 8px grid. Angles are 0, 45, and 90. Depth is only opacity and overlap: no shadow, no lighting gradient, no filter, no particle mesh. Seeded randomness chooses among legal placements. It does not invent new marks.

Numbers are derived, never eyeballed. The frame is 320x200, an 8:5 Fibonacci rectangle. Its golden sections, snapped to the grid, give four crossings at (120, 80), (200, 80), (120, 120), and (200, 120); every still centres its focal mark on one of them, and which one is decided by additive recurrence, `frac(n / phi)`, so ten focal positions distribute evenly instead of by preference. Every device is contained in one drawn square, the plate: 168x168, stroke 2, mute on hue fields and cream on neutral fields. 168 is itself a Fibonacci square (64 + 104 = 168), so its internal golden sections land exactly on the grid, and the plate is placed, at (56, 16) or (96, 16), so the still's focal anchor coincides with one of the plate's own crossings. Device ink keeps 8px of clearance inside; nothing touches the boundary. Sizes come from Fibonacci times 8: 8, 16, 24, 40, 64, 104, 168. Strokes are 1, 2, 3, 5. Opacity climbs one ladder, the powers of `1/phi`: 1.0, 0.62, 0.38, 0.24, 0.15, 0.09, and it always encodes meaning (decay, uncertainty, recession), never polish. Emptiness is a measured parameter, not an intention: painted area stays at or under `64000 / phi^2`, so at least 61.8% of the frame is untouched and the still survives as a thumbnail and as a sibling to type. Phi is used here as a constraint generator, for its additive scale, its exact complementarity, and its low-discrepancy distribution. It is not a beauty claim, and it never overrides the grid.

Category identity is a force, never a costume. Six operators do the composing: containment (a boundary creates belonging), sequence (an ordered run with one lit member), vector (one directed force that travels and stops), reduction (overlap resolving into one simple mark), decay (a monotonic opacity ramp), relay (centres joined by thin rules passing influence). Ten categories share those six. Hue, anchor, and parameters make the difference; style never does.

Colour follows one recipe in two modes. In mode A, the category hue mixed at 38.2% over ink becomes the field, the field mixed at 38.2% over ink again becomes the mute, and cream marks the single active element. In mode B, reserved for violet and indigo, the field is a neutral grey, the mute is cream diluted at 23.6% into it, and the category hue lands on exactly one element. Violet and indigo never become ground. One fraction generates every field and mute in the library, so the palette cannot drift, and the measured floors hold everywhere: marks clear 4.5:1 against their field, mutes stay recessive above 1.5:1, mode B accents clear 3:1.

One artwork serves both themes. The still carries its own field, so it reads as a dark plate on light paper and as a soft-edged plate on dark editorial chrome. There is no light twin and no dark twin, no theme-swapped duplicate, and no CSS variable inside the art: colours are baked literal hex, because the file is a fixed plate, not a themeable component. Grayscale must still separate every still by silhouette. Variation is parametric and reproducible: a seed hashes from `aup-editorial-v1|categoryId|hex`, and re-running a generator produces byte-identical files. Changing the seed rearranges the same grammar. Changing the category rearranges the parameters, not the style.

The probe viewer exists so a human can keep, tweak, or kill a direction before the library inherits it. Beauty lives in the procedure. Each run should feel like a print pulled from a tuned plate: sparse, aligned, and finished, the look of countless iterations rather than a lucky frame.

# Inspiration board: Master symbol alternative directions

**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Agent:** inspiration-scout  
**Project:** Agentic UX Patterns (`aux-patterns`)  
**Audience:** creative director (owner), then design-lead  
**Mode:** Alt-direction scout (compete with locked seal; do not redraw)

---

## 1. Scout brief

**Job:** Find aesthetic and construction references that help invent **two additional master-symbol directions**, each still about agentic UX and human hold containing agency, but visually and conceptually distinct from the current keyhole-in-ring seal.

**Feel target (shared with locked mark):** Quiet authority + Soft museum object. A citation stamp that can live in the same room as the teaching stills (cream `#F2EEE2`, night paper `#0e0f12`, flat vector, generous empty field).

**Must keep:** Human-in-the-loop as the metaphor spine. Independent identity (no Gemframe purple, no client brand, no Laws of UX Euclid nest: circle-square-triangle). Survivable at 16px favicon and poster scale. Category hues stay out of the seal.

**Must leave:** SaaS purple glow, AI robot clichés, glossy 3D, LoUX twin geometry, closed jewelry-seal thinking as the only option.

**Stills grammar (optional ally):** 224 square, flat primitives, cream marks, φ/8px thinking, empty field. Use when the mark should feel like a sibling of the stills. Argue **desk chrome** divergence when the seal should feel like site chrome rather than a teaching still.

---

## 2. Current mark as baseline (anti-clone)

**Locked construction:** Thick rounded frame + circle + stem through bottom (keyhole / pin). Dark + light modes, subtle top fade, luminosity on the hold layer. Component set `aux-master-symbol` (Figma file `g81CTKZNVjYAqhjdVEPji4`, node ~`89:559` / P1 lock notes in `design-state.md`).

**What to keep if competing marks win later:** The emotional taste (quiet authority, museum object). Dual-mode cream/night. Human hold containing agency. One geometric master symbol (Laws-of-UX *job*, not Laws-of-UX *clone*).

**What to leave behind when exploring A/B:** Continuous ring as the only hold. Keyhole stem as the only “loop” cue. Jewelry-seal silhouette that reads as pin/lock hardware first. Any path that becomes a LoUX nest or a purple brand canvas.

This board does **not** redraw the logo. It hands the creative director transferable principles and named directions to invent from.

## 3. Direction A: Open Caliper

**One-sentence idea:** Human oversight is an **open measuring grip** (two jaws / brackets) that holds agency between them without sealing it into a ring; release and re-grip stay visible in the silhouette.

### Why it differs from the current keyhole seal

- No continuous loop, no bottom stem, no keyhole silhouette.
- Openness is the hero: the hold can widen, tighten, or release (HITL as calibrated grip, not vault lock).
- Reads as instrument / editorial bracket, not jewelry seal.
- Can sit closer to stills grammar (two offset cream strokes + one filled kernel on empty field) **or** as thin desk chrome beside serif titles.

### References (borrow / avoid)

#### 1. Whitney Museum “responsive W” (Experimental Jetset, 2013)

- **Links:** [whitney.org/about/new-identity](https://whitney.org/about/new-identity), [Experimental Jetset archive](https://jetset.nl/archive/whitney-museum-identity), [Dezeen coverage](https://www.dezeen.com/2013/05/21/whitney-graphic-identity-by-experimental-jetset/)
- **Borrow:** Open line architecture that *frames* content without becoming a filled badge. Institutional calm from geometry + type, not from a closed emblem. The idea that the mark is a hold for other material.
- **Avoid:** Zigzag W letterform, responsive morphing system complexity, Helvetica-as-museum-default as *our* voice (site already owns Iowan/Palatino).

#### 2. National Geographic yellow border (frame-as-identity)

- **Links:** [National Geographic (magazine history)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Geographic_Magazine), [Sviiter: yellow frame as logo](https://sviiter.studio/blog/famous-logos-xxiii-national-geographic)
- **Borrow:** Identity as a **structure that holds** something else. Extreme simplicity that survives tiny and poster scales. Window metaphor: human frame, world (or agent) inside.
- **Avoid:** Yellow brand canvas, photo-hero dependency, closed continuous rectangle as the *only* read (for Direction A prefer broken / open jaws, not a full picture frame).

#### 3. Chase Manhattan octagon (Tom Geismar / Chermayeff & Geismar, 1961)

- **Links:** [CGH project page](https://cghnyc.com/work/project/chase-bank), [Logo Histories write-up](https://www.logohistories.com/p/chase-manhattan-bank-logo-1960s-chermayeff)
- **Borrow:** Proof that abstract geometry can carry “trust / hold / center” without illustration. Designed to survive newspaper black-and-white and cufflink scale (favicon lesson).
- **Avoid:** Closed octagon vault, corporate blue, square-in-polygon that drifts toward LoUX nesting or toward the locked keyhole’s “closed container” family. Use as contrast: *this* is the closed cousin we are *not* cloning for Direction A.

#### 4. Otl Aicher, Munich 1972 pictogram system

- **Links:** [Cooper Hewitt note](https://www.cooperhewitt.org/2017/12/29/faster-higher-stronger/), [Smithsonian Mag overview](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/this-graphic-artists-olympic-pictograms-changed-urban-design-forever-180978256/)
- **Borrow:** Modular strokes, language-light geometry, shared alphabet so a “jaw” and a “kernel” feel related to stills primitives. Caption lives beside the mark, not inside it.
- **Avoid:** Sports-body vocabulary, pure black austerity as brand, Olympic colour system.

#### 5. Typographic brackets / editorial punctuation (cross-domain wild card)

- **Links:** Swiss Style / International Typographic Style overview via [Helvetica history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helvetica); foundry craft tone via [Klim Type Foundry](https://klim.co.nz/) and [Commercial Type](https://commercialtype.com/) (systems discipline, not their logotypes as marks to copy)
- **Borrow:** Parentheses / brackets as a cultural shorthand for “held material.” Night Reference Desk already speaks in citation voice; an open-bracket seal would rhyme with editorial chrome without becoming a wordmark.
- **Avoid:** Literal `()` characters as the logo, Helvetica monoculture, foundry-brand cloning.

### Accessibility / tiny-size notes (Direction A)

- At 16px, two facing jaws + center kernel must remain **three separable masses**; if jaws thin out, the mark becomes a broken “C” or equals-sign noise.
- Prefer filled strokes over hairlines; test cream-on-night and night-on-cream.
- Open silhouette helps distinguish from the locked keyhole in a favicon strip, which is good for A/B probes.
- Do not encode meaning in category hue; keep Gallery Cream / night paper only.

### Stills vs desk chrome

Prefer **stills sibling** for Open Caliper: empty field, cream jaws, one offset filled agent chip. Argue desk chrome only if the jaws become thin rules that fight the stills’ filled language.

## 4. Direction B: Punched Plate

**One-sentence idea:** Human policy is a **stamped plate** (filled mass) whose **counterform void** is the hold; agency is a small filled chip that lives only inside the punched aperture (accession stamp, not jewelry seal).

### Why it differs from the current keyhole seal

- Rectangular (or soft-square) **mass + punch**, not ring + stem.
- Metaphor shifts from keyhole/lock to **library accession / printer’s device / museum stamp**.
- Counterform is the hold: negative space does the HITL work; the plate is human authority as object.
- Orthogonal to Open Caliper: A is open air between jaws; B is closed mass with a hole.

### Why it differs from Direction A

| | Open Caliper | Punched Plate |
|--|--------------|---------------|
| Air vs mass | Mostly empty field | Mostly filled plate |
| Hold geometry | Positive jaws | Negative aperture |
| Object type | Instrument / bracket | Stamp / seal plate |
| Risk if weak | Reads as UI chevrons | Reads as app icon hole or Chase coin |

### References (borrow / avoid)

#### 1. Chase coin / vault geometry (counterform lesson, not brand)

- **Links:** [CGH Chase](https://cghnyc.com/work/project/chase-bank), [Logo Histories](https://www.logohistories.com/p/chase-manhattan-bank-logo-1960s-chermayeff)
- **Borrow:** Chinese-coin lineage: **mass with a punched center** as “secure hold.” Tiny-size survival of a bold counterform. Abstract trust without a mascot.
- **Avoid:** Octagon brand silhouette, bank-vault story as ours, blue field, square-in-octagon that reintroduces Euclid nesting. For AUX, punch an aperture that is *not* a centered square clone and *not* a keyhole.

#### 2. Penguin Books colophon (Edward Young / Jan Tschichold / Angus Hyland)

- **Links:** [Creative Review: Penguin logo](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/penguin-logo-edward-young/), [Design Museum: Penguin Books](https://designmuseum.org/penguin-books), [Logo Design Love evolution](https://www.logodesignlove.com/penguin-logo)
- **Borrow:** Citation mark that lives *with teaching content* (spine / cover), dignified but not corporate. Proof that a small master mark can carry disproportionate authority next to pattern/teaching surfaces. Series discipline over decades.
- **Avoid:** Animal mascot, orange lozenge as brand colour system, “cute” anthropomorphism (robot-adjacent risk in AI contexts).

#### 3. Aldus Manutius printer’s device (dolphin & anchor): cultural archetype

- **Links:** [Cambridge exhibition: Dante device](https://exhibitions.lib.cam.ac.uk/manutius/artifacts/dante-commedia-device/), [Grolier Club: dolphin and anchor](https://grolierclub.omeka.net/exhibits/show/aldus-manutius/dolphin-and-anchor), [UCD: printer’s marks](https://ucdculturalheritagecollections.com/2023/06/29/delightful-devices-the-art-of-the-printers-mark/)
- **Borrow:** The *job* of a printer’s device: stamp of origin, quality, and editorial care. Frame around a device (Aldus often set the mark in a ruled rectangle). Motto energy of “hasten slowly” maps emotionally to human-in-the-loop (speed of agents, deliberation of humans) without painting it.
- **Avoid:** Dolphin/anchor figurative clone, Renaissance clutter, Latin motto inside the mark.

#### 4. Bauhaus signet (Oskar Schlemmer, 1922)

- **Links:** [People’s Graphic Design Archive](https://peoplesgdarchive.org/item/16318/bauhaus-emblem-1922), [Wikimedia: Bauhaus-Signet](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bauhaus-Signet.svg), [Schlemmer Mint: signet story](https://mint.schlemmer.org/about)
- **Borrow:** Seal-as-belonging for a teaching school. Geometric reduction of “human” into planar modules. Circular *stamp culture* as institutional object.
- **Avoid:** Profile-face signet clone, Bauhaus nostalgia as AUX brand, dense linework that dies at 16px.

#### 5. National Geographic border + stills 224 plate (desk chrome argument)

- **Links:** Mag border history above; project stills grammar in `design-state.md` and `DESIGN.md` (cream `#F2EEE2`, 224 squares)
- **Borrow:** Rectangular plate as the natural “desk object” next to Night Reference Desk chrome. Filled soft-square with a punched void can share the stills’ module without being a teaching still.
- **Avoid:** Yellow NatGeo canvas, photo window dependency, treating the plate as a second LoUX card.

### Accessibility / tiny-size notes (Direction B)

- Counterform aperture needs enough area at 16px to stay a hole, not a speck; agent chip inside needs ≥1–2px clear air from the punch edge (8px grid thinking).
- Filled plate helps poster scale and favicon mass; risk is “generic app icon.” Offset the punch (stills family: filled, offset) so it does not read as a centered OS glyph.
- Test luminosity: cream plate on night paper vs night plate with cream punch; ensure ≥3:1 for the aperture edge.
- Avoid relying on the locked mark’s top fade gradient; let mass/void carry the read.

### Stills vs desk chrome

Strong case for **desk chrome divergence:** Punched Plate is a stamp that sits *beside* stills, not among them. Keep stills cues (cream, flat, offset, φ padding) but accept a heavier ink budget than teaching stills’ ≥61.8% empty rule.

## 6. Recommended next probe set (for the human in Figma)

Draw **light probes only** (cream `#F2EEE2` on `#0e0f12` and inverse). No category hues. No redraw of P1 keyhole except as a control tile in the same strip.

1. **A1 Parallel jaws:** Two thick vertical (or slightly arced) cream bars; one filled offset disc between; generous side air.
2. **A2 Caliper tips:** Jaws meet a shared hinge at top or bottom (hinge = human pivot); kernel floats in the opening; test whether hinge steals the read from HITL.
3. **A3 Bracket pair:** Editorial `⌈ ⌋` or soft L-brackets as filled strokes; kernel centered; compare to A1 for “instrument vs punctuation.”
4. **B1 Soft-square plate + oval punch:** Offset oval void; small cream chip inside void; plate radius near stills language.
5. **B2 Plate + asymmetric aperture:** Punch shifted toward a φ anchor (not centered); chip offset inside punch (stills “filled, offset” family).
6. **B3 Plate + rectangular punch:** Stronger accession-stamp read; watch Chase/LoUX nest risk; keep punch proportions non-Euclidean relative to outer plate.
7. **Strip test:** 16 / 32 / 64 / 224 on night and on cream paper; place Current | A-winner | B-winner in one row for a look-then-pick review (same ritual as `docs/designpowers/probes/2026-08-17-master-symbol-paths.html`).

**Do not probe yet:** gradients as meaning, category hue fills, figurative hands, robots, ∞ glyphs, LoUX nest geometry.

## 7. Taste and accessibility summary

**Taste alignment:** Both directions aim at Quiet authority + Soft museum object. A leans instrument/editorial; B leans stamp/citation. Neither needs purple, glow, or mascot.

**Filtered out (interesting, wrong here):**

- Whitney morphing system (process inspiration only).
- NatGeo yellow as brand colour.
- Chase octagon as silhouette.
- Penguin bird / Aldus dolphin as figures.
- Bauhaus face signet as clone.
- SaaS AI “orb + circuit” marks (not listed as refs; rejected on brief).

**A11y watch:** High figure/ground on cream/night; meaning in silhouette not colour; 16px separability of hold vs agent; if a probe fails grayscale, reject it before liking it in colour.
